UncleMelo is a calm music player for the files you already have. It lives at [music.thereflectivefootball.com](https://music.thereflectivefootball.com).

Open that link in Chrome on a computer and choose Build my library. Point it at a folder of music. UncleMelo reads the songs on that computer and plays them there. Friends can open a shared mix link, then get UncleMelo for their own music.

No music is ever uploaded. Files stay where they are.

Part of [The Reflective Football](https://thereflectivefootball.com).
